How to read this · method

Sun hours are accumulated direct sun, not total daylight or solar energy. A brighter cell means more sampled time in direct sun within the chosen window. Compare different dates using the same area and time window to explore seasonal change. Results use mapped buildings and canopies; roofs are simplified planes. Clouds, diffuse and reflected light are not included, and rough LiDAR roof detail is not treated as engineering-grade solar geometry.

How to read this · method and limits

Traffic mode uses road-by-road SUMO HBEFA3 NOx as a gas-like fume tracer, then releases equal-mass parcels from those road links into one steady OpenFOAM wind field. The plume colours blend smoothly while percentile bands rank positive map cells within this run; they are not absolute pollution grades or µg/m³. Soft animated smoke puffs visualise parcel paths and are not a resolved concentration cloud. Blank areas may have no recorded plume or only trace below the display cutoff; neither establishes clean air. Demand is synthetic unless observed edge counts are configured. This exploratory screen does not predict health risk or the effect of planting trees.

Method & limitations

Direction displays the full-CBD steady RANS k–ε OpenFOAM case for the selected wind direction, including terrain-following pedestrian speed, vertical velocity, pressure and turbulence for all mapped buildings. SE 135° and NW 315° are solved today; other directions will appear as their cases are meshed, solved and converted. Comfort weights each solved OpenFOAM direction's pedestrian-height field by its ERA5 wind-rose frequency and Weibull exceedance; directions without a solved case are excluded from the weighted sum rather than interpolated or assumed calm, so today's comfort result is a lower-bound estimate covering only the resolved sector(s), not a complete annual study. Neither mode has passed domain/mesh sensitivity or field validation and must not be used for certification.
